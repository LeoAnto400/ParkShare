    Earnings

    Completed, confirmed paid bookings earn the owner their stored parking subtotal. New listed prices include the platform fee: 5% of the driver total goes to the platform and the remainder to the owner. Historical bookings retain their original stored split. Upcoming and active paid reservations remain pending completion. Refunded, unpaid, failed and cancelled reservations do not count as earnings.
